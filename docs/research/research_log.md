# ROADLINK – Research #

Overview
This folder contains research supporting the proposed ROADLINK system.

Research Focus
- Road disruption reporting
- Transport coordination
- Existing reporting systems
- Rwanda road data and statistics
- Oracle Database and PL/SQL

## Research Sources
I reviewed academic papers, official reports, an existing system, a road-network dataset, and Oracle documentation.



## Purpose
The research helps me understand the problem, identify existing solutions, and plan the ROADLINK system.

## Research Conclusion

The sources show that crowdsourcing can support road reporting, but submitted information needs verification. I plan to use these findings to guide ROADLINK's reporting, officer verification, and transport coordination features.

**Note:** The RTDA dataset contains road-network information, not live incidents. Any sample incident records will be identified as test data.
